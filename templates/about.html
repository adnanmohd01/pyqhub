Overview & History ──

Invertis University, Bareilly is one of the best private universities in India that focuses on holistic life skills, professional development, and high-quality education to produce exceptional students who consistently achieve outstanding academic results.

Through a powerful combination of experiential learning, interactions with industry leaders, exposure to best practices, and inspired teaching from faculty with strong academic credentials, the University provides students with the opportunity to develop mastery of their subject.

We offer a range of Doctoral, Post Graduate, Undergraduate & Diploma programmes in Engineering & Technology, Management, Computer Applications, Food Technology, Law, Education, Journalism & Mass Communication, Bio-Science & Commerce, Pharmacy, Technology, Science, Fashion Design and Agriculture.

Vision & Mission ──

Strengths ──

Quality of Education

We are committed to providing students with quality education and reliable academic resources that support their learning and overall development. Our platform offers previous year question papers, study materials, important questions, and other useful resources to help students understand exam patterns, focus on important topics, practice effectively, and prepare with confidence. We aim to make education more accessible, convenient, and student-friendly while helping students achieve their academic goals and build a strong foundation for their future careers.

Research Opportunities

We believe in promoting quality research that encourages innovation, creativity, and the discovery of new knowledge. Our aim is to support meaningful research in engineering, science, management, and humanities while developing critical thinking and practical solutions. Through research and collaboration, we strive to contribute valuable ideas that address real-world challenges and benefit society.

Experienced Faculty

Our faculty members come from diverse educational and professional backgrounds, bringing valuable knowledge, experience, and practical insights to the classroom. Their expertise helps students understand concepts more effectively, develop critical thinking skills, and gain practical knowledge that prepares them for academic success and future career opportunities

Industry and Academic Collaborations

We have established strong industry connections with reputed companies, providing students with valuable exposure to real-world practices and career opportunities. We also promote student exchange programmes with internationally recognized universities and colleges, enabling students to gain global exposure, experience diverse learning environments, and broaden their academic and professional perspectives.
